> [!IMPORTANT]
> **TrueParser is Currently in Beta.** During this period, the platform is **free to use** for all approved tenants. We offer a generous **Beta Plan** that allows for high-volume technical parsing.
> 
> Please note that as a beta product, the system may have quirks and is **not yet suitable for critical or production-sensitive data**.

Every app in TrueParser should be linked to a plan to be functional. 

- **No Plan Assigned**: Your app can still issue tokens, but they will not contain the processing limits or permissions required to parse documents.
- **Retired Plan Assigned**: Token issuance is blocked. You must switch to an active plan to restore access.

---

## Beta Plan

To support developers during our launch phase, we offer a generous Beta tier:

- **Unlimited Access**: Each account can assign the Beta plan to their applications.
- **High Thresholds**: The Beta plan includes significantly higher parsing limits than our future standard tiers to encourage thorough testing.
- **Feedback**: We encourage reporting any issues or "quirks" to our team to help us reach production stability.

---

## Assigning or Changing a Plan

You can change an app's plan at any time through the **Plans** tab in the dashboard.

### The Process

1. Select your target plan.
2. If moving to a paid plan for the first time, you will provide billing details via our secure provider.
3. Once the plan is confirmed, the dashboard updates your app's metadata and synchronizes the new limits.

---

## Understanding Proration

TrueParser uses standard SaaS billing logic to ensure you only pay for what you use:

- **Free to Paid**: the change happens immediately, and you are charged for the upcoming billing cycle.
- **Paid to Paid**: the change is prorated immediately based on usage.

---

## Subscription Status

You can monitor your subscription health in the **Billing** section:

| Status | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Active** | Everything is running smoothly. |
| **Past Due** | A payment attempt failed. You have a grace period to update your card. |
| **Cancelled** | The app remains active until the end of the current period. |

<Info>
  Disabling or deleting an app does not automatically cancel a paid subscription. Always manage subscription status in the Billing tab before deleting resources.
</Info>
