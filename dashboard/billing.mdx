Every app in TrueParser must be linked to a plan. The plan determines the resource limits available to that specific application.

---

## Free plan

To support developers while maintaining system stability, TrueParser offers a generous Free tier with the following rule:

- Each tenant is allowed exactly one application on a Free plan.
- If you attempt to assign a Free plan to a second app, the dashboard will block the request. You must either upgrade the existing Free app to a paid tier or delete it before creating a new one.

---

## Assigning or changing a plan

You can change an app's plan at any time through the Plans tab in the dashboard.

### The process

1. Select your new target plan.
2. If moving to a paid plan for the first time, you will be redirected to checkout to provide billing details.
3. Once the plan is confirmed, the dashboard updates your app's metadata and pushes the new limits to the managed sync layer.

---

## Understanding proration

TrueParser uses standard SaaS billing logic to ensure you only pay for what you use:

- Free to paid: the change happens immediately, and you are charged the full amount for the upcoming billing cycle.
- Paid to paid: the change is prorated immediately.

---

## Subscription status

You can monitor your subscription health in the Billing section:

- Active: Everything is running smoothly.
- Past due: A payment attempt failed. You have a grace period to update your card before the app is automatically disabled.
- Cancelled: The app remains active until the end of the current billing period, after which it reverts to an inactive state.

<Info>
  Disabling or deleting an app does not automatically cancel a paid subscription. Always manage subscription status in the Billing tab before deleting resources.
</Info>
