---
title: "License Regions"
description: "How to choose the correct License Region for your application."
---

During the creation of your App, you must select a **License Region**. This is one of the most important architectural decisions for your project.

A License Region is a specific geographic area (e.g., `aws-us-east-1`) where TrueParser maintains a dedicated presence and data storage cluster.

---

### Why Does It Matter?

1.  **Data Residency**: All metadata associated with your App—including billing logs, plan limits, and routing info—is stored in isolated infrastructure within that specific geography.
2.  **Latency**: For the best performance, you should select the region closest to your primary user base. This minimizes response times when your backend requests security tokens from the dashboard.
3.  **Regulatory Compliance**: If your project must adhere to data sovereignty laws (like GDPR in Europe), selecting the correct License Region ensures your management data never leaves that geographic boundary.

---

## Available License Regions

| Code                 | Location              |
| :------------------- | :-------------------- |
| `aws-ap-northeast-1` | Asia Pacific (Tokyo)  |
| `aws-ap-south-1`     | Asia Pacific (Mumbai) |
| `aws-eu-west-1`      | Europe (Ireland)      |
| `aws-us-east-1`      | US East (N. Virginia) |
| `aws-us-east-2`      | US East (Ohio)        |
| `aws-us-west-2`      | US West (Oregon)      |

### Can I Change My Region?

<Warning>
  **The License Region is a "set-once" property.** Because your App's data is
  physically provisioned in regional storage, it cannot be migrated to another region after the App is created.
</Warning>

If you need to change regions, you must create a new App and migrate your user traffic to the new credentials.
