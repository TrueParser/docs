This page describes the developer onboarding flow. It guides you from creating your organization to using the Developer Dashboard, configuring credentials, and issuing your first machine token.

---

## 1. Register a Tenant Account (New Organizations)

If you are setting up a new organization or business context on the platform, start by registering a **Tenant Account**.

*   Navigate to [dashboard.trueparser.com](https://dashboard.trueparser.com).
*   Provide your **Company Name**, **Admin Email Address**, and a secure **Password**.
*   Upon form submission, the backend initializes your organization's context, pre-warms your asymmetric key material, and schedules your welcome email.
*   By default, new self-registered tenants are queued in a `Pending Activation` state.

![Tenant Registration Screen](/images/onboarding/tenant-signup.png)

---

## 2. Wait for Tenant Activation

To protect platform resources, new tenant applications cannot immediately issue machine-to-machine (M2M) credentials.

Depending on the system's runtime configuration, activation is processed in one of two ways:
*   **Auto-Activation via Email (GA Flow)**: You will receive an activation email containing a secure token. Clicking the link takes you to the frontend `/activate` page, which validates the token and activates the tenant instantly.
*   **Manual Activation (Beta Flow)**: A platform administrator verifies your request and manually enables your tenant. 

Once activated, your primary administrator account is enabled, and a billing customer context is established.

---

## 3. Team Member Invitation & Onboarding (User Account Creation)

If you are joining an existing organization, you **cannot** publicly register. An administrator in your tenant must invite you using the **User Management** screen.

1.  The administrator enters your email and assigns a security role (e.g., `Developer` or `Admin`).
2.  The backend generates a secure token and queues an invitation email.
3.  Clicking the link in the invitation email takes you to the **User Account Creation** screen to complete your profile registration.

![User Account Creation Screen](/images/onboarding/user-account-creation.png)

---

## 4. Password Security & Password Resets

If you or a team member forgets a password, use the password reset flow. 

*   Enter your registered email address on the sign-in page to request a reset link.
*   The system generates a time-limited token and delivers a branded password reset email.
*   Open the link to load the secure reset page where you can safely apply your new credentials.

## 5. Dashboard Sign-In

Once your tenant is activated or you have successfully registered your user profile via a team invitation, you can sign in to the control plane.

*   Navigate to the sign-in interface at [dashboard.trueparser.com](https://dashboard.trueparser.com).
*   **Standard Sign-In**: Authenticate securely using your registered Email and Password.
*   **External Authentication (SSO)**: If your organization uses corporate accounts, select **Sign in with Google** to authenticate via Google SSO (pre-configured at the host level).

![Developer Sign In Screen](/images/onboarding/login.png)

---

## 6. Explore the Developer Dashboard (v1)

Once your account is active and you sign in, you are greeted by the **TrueParser Developer Dashboard (v1)**. This central hub is divided into core navigation tiles and helper sections.

![TrueParser Developer Dashboard](/images/onboarding/dashboard.png)

### The Core Management Tiles:
*   **Application Management**: Create, enable/disable, and configure machine identities (Apps) for your backends.
*   **User Management**: Invite team members, track invitation status (e.g., `Pending`, `Accepted`, `Expired`), and manage role assignments.
*   **Set Up Profile**: Configure your personal user details and security parameters.
*   **Billing**: View your current subscription level and assigned plan quotas.

### Utility Panels & System Info:
*   **Recent Jobs**: Monitor recent document-parsing tasks, status outputs (`Success`, `Failed`), and processing statistics.
*   **Documentation Portal**: Quick links to developers' guides, API contracts, and integration tutorials.
*   **System Status**: Real-time liveness and readiness status indicator for control plane services and data plane nodes.
*   **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**: Curated assistance for common setup issues, storage configs, and key rotations.

## 7. Create Your First App

An **App** represents a secure confidential client identity that your backend microservices or the Demo UI will use to negotiate OAuth 2.0 access tokens.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Application Management">
    Click the **Application Management** tile on your dashboard and select **Create App**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a License Region">
    Choose a valid deployment region (e.g., `aws-us-east-1`). The backend resolves this to the closest active database shard and stamps your app record with a `DbCode`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Allowed Domains (Optional)">
    You can specify up to three allowed domains (e.g., `localhost:3000` or `api.acme.com`). The control plane normalizes these URLs and includes them as security claims in your issued JWTs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Secure Your Plaintext Secret">
    Upon creation, the dashboard will display your `client_id` and a one-time plaintext `client_secret`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **The `client_secret` is only shown once.** Save it securely in your backend secret manager (like Infiscal, AWS Secrets Manager, or HashiCorp Vault). If you lose it, you must regenerate it from the dashboard, which immediately invalidates the old credential.
</Warning>

## 8. Optional: Connect Custom S3 Storage

By default, TrueParser securely manages parsed document artifacts. If your business rules or compliance guidelines require data residency within your own boundary:

1.  Open your App settings in the dashboard.
2.  Provide your S3-compatible credentials (supports **AWS S3**, **Tigris**, **Cloudflare R2**, **MinIO**, and **Backblaze B2**).
3.  The control plane encrypts these credentials with a master key and uses them on your behalf to write parser outputs directly to your bucket.

---

## 9. Assign a Plan for Entitlement Quotas

Although applications can negotiate basic access tokens without an explicitly assigned plan, plan assignments govern your usage boundaries:

*   **No Plan Assigned**: Tokens are successfully issued, but they lack the `entitlements` claim.
*   **Active Plan Assigned**: Access tokens are stamped with `entitlements` containing your allowed Document Units and feature flags.
*   **Retired Plan Assigned**: Access tokens are immediately blocked at the server level.

<Note>
  **Beta Quota Rule**: During the beta period, paid tiers are feature-gated. Tenants can assign a single free plan to **only one app** at a time.
</Note>

## 10. Request Your First Token from the Backend

Now that your tenant is active, your admin account is configured, and your app is created, your backend can call the token endpoint using the OAuth 2.0 `client_credentials` grant.

### Example Token Exchange Request:

```bash
curl -X POST https://admin-api.trueparser.com/connect/token \
  -H "Content-Type: application/x-www-form-urlencoded" \
  -d "grant_type=client_credentials" \
  -d "client_id=YOUR_CLIENT_ID" \
  -d "client_secret=YOUR_CLIENT_SECRET" \
  -d "scope=TrueParser.API"
```

The returned JWT access token contains the essential routing claims (`tenantid`, `appId`, `licenseRegionCode`, `dbCode`) along with any plan entitlements needed to start parsing.
