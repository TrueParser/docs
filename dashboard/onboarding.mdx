TrueParser uses a tenant-isolated orchestration model. This guide reflects the actual onboarding workflow as implemented in the platform.

---

## 1. Create Your Tenant Account

Create your tenant account at [dashboard.trueparser.com](https://dashboard.trueparser.com).

- **Authentication**: The current setup supports **Email and Password** sign-in for human administrators.
- **Initial State**: New tenant registrations start in a **Pending Activation** state.
- **Welcome Email**: A welcome email is queued immediately upon registration to confirm your account is in our queue.

---

## 2. Wait for Tenant Activation

To maintain platform stability, new tenants require a one-time activation.

- **Status Change**: Activation is handled as a separate administrative step.
- **Activation Email**: When your tenant is activated, you will receive an automated email with your login details.

---

## 3. Create Your First App

An App contains the configuration and credentials your backend uses to authenticate and connect to the TrueParser API.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Application">
    Click on **Apps** in the sidebar and select **Add Application**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Region & Data Residency">
    Provide a name (e.g., `production-parser`) and select your **License
    Region**. TrueParser automatically handles the regional isolation for your
    app's metadata.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Security Configuration">
    In the app settings, add your **Allowed Domains** (e.g., `localhost:3000`,
    `myapp.com`). This is a critical security step that protects your application **usage quota**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture Credentials">
    Once created, you will see your `client_id` and `client_secret`. **Copy them
    immediately.**
    <Warning>
      The `client_secret` is only shown once. If lost, you must regenerate it,
      which invalidates any service currently using the old secret.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## 4. Choose a License Region

Selecting a **License Region** is a mandatory step during application creation.

- **Database Stamping**: The system resolves your chosen region to a physical **License Region** record.
- **Active Shard Requirement**: App creation requires an active database shard for that region. If no active database is available, creation will fail.
- **Technical Identifier**: The resolved database code is stamped onto your application. You will see this identifier (`dbCode`) in your issued access tokens.

---

## 5. Configure Allowed Domains

Allowed domains are stored as security metadata to prevent unauthorized third-party cross-origin requests.

- **Normalization**: The platform automatically cleans your domain entries (stripping protocols and trailing slashes).
- **Validation**: Wildcards (`*`) are not supported, but `localhost` (with port) is permitted.
- **Capacity**: You can configure up to three unique domains.

---

## 6. Understand Token Issuance

A tenant application can issue security tokens only when the platform's safety criteria are met. Unlike Dashboard access, API authentication uses the Identity Authority endpoint.

1. The App must be **Enabled**.
2. The Tenant must be **Active**.
3. The App must have a **License Region** and **Database Code** assigned.

<Note>
  **Plan Behavior.** An application can always issue a token. However, to receive the usage quotas (limits) required for the Parsing API, the application must be linked to an active plan in the dashboard.
</Note>

---

## 7. Manage Secrets Safely

You must store your application's `client_secret` securely.

- **Regeneration**: If a secret is lost or compromised, you must use the **Regenerate client_secret** flow in the dashboard.
- **Instant Invalidation**: Regenerating a secret issues a new one immediately and invalidates the old one. Any services using the previous secret will lose access until updated.

---

## 8. Assigning a Plan

Plans determine the usage quotas injected into your tokens.

- **The One Free Rule**: Each organization is limited to exactly **one** application on a Free plan.
- **Scaling**: Paid plans use our integrated billing flow and affect the `limits` claim in your issued tokens.
