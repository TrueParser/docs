## Overview

TrueParser applies plan-based limits to keep usage predictable and fair across a tenant.

These limits are enforced across the tenant and apply to all apps attached to that tenant.

<Card title="Important">
  Tenants that need higher throughput or more concurrency should consider

  [trueparser.com/contact](https://trueparser.com/contact).
</Card>

Depending on your plan, limits can be applied over multiple windows:

- per minute
- per day
- per week
- per month

The exact values depend on your plan.

## Request Limits

TrueParser enforces request limits for API traffic.

At a high level:

- request limits are enforced per tenant
- limits are shared across all apps attached to the same tenant
- exceeding a limit returns `429 Too Many Requests`

## Upload Protections

TrueParser also applies upload protections to keep large or bursty traffic from overwhelming the platform.

These protections help balance:

- concurrency
- request bursts
- upload bandwidth

At the product level, tenants are protected by caps on concurrent uploads, request rate, and upload bandwidth.

We do not expose the internal enforcement details, but the platform uses these controls together with plan-based limits to keep upload behavior fair.

## Upload Size Support

The platform supports mixed upload sizes up to a maximum of `25 MB` per upload, subject to your plan.

That means a tenant may upload:

- `1 KB` files
- `1 MB` files
- `25 MB` files

The request limits and upload protections work together so small uploads and large uploads are both handled fairly.

## Plan-Based Usage Windows

In addition to upload limits, TrueParser plans may include usage windows that reset over time.

These windows may include:

- per minute
- per day
- per week
- per month

The exact combination of windows depends on the plan you are on.

## Recommended Baseline

For a `25 MB` maximum upload size, a good starting point is:

- plan-based limits set according to your tenant tier
- upload protections enabled by default
- maximum upload size of `25 MB`

This gives enough headroom for mixed traffic while still keeping upload pressure under control.

## Notes

- Limits are tenant-scoped.
- All apps for the same tenant share the same limits.
- Tenant plans may include caps on concurrent uploads, request rate, and upload bandwidth.
- The exact values can be tuned later as workloads change.
- If a request exceeds a limit, the platform returns `429 Too Many Requests`.

If the platform is temporarily overloaded, it may also return `503 Service Unavailable`, and you should retry with backoff when appropriate.
