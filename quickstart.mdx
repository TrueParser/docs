## 1. Sign up

Create your TrueParser account at [dashboard.trueparser.com](https://dashboard.trueparser.com).

- **Authentication**: TrueParser currently supports **Email and Password** sign-in.
- **Initial State**: Your account will start in a **Pending Activation** state while our team processes your registration. You will receive an email once your account is active.

---

## 2. Create an App

To connect to TrueParser, you need a security token. Tokens are issued through an OAuth 2 machine-to-machine flow. First, create an app in the dashboard to generate your credentials.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Apps section">
    Navigate to **Apps** in the dashboard sidebar and click **Add Application**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your app">
    Identify the service connecting to TrueParser, for example `my-legal-analyzer-prod`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the License Region">
    Choose the geographic region closest to your primary data region (e.g., `aws-us-east-1`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your credentials">
    Immediately copy your **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. The secret is shown in plaintext only at this step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## 3. Recommended: Activate a Plan

To parse technical documents, your app needs the **usage quota** provided by a plan. While you can obtain a token without a plan, it will be rejected by the system as it lacks the required permissions. Select a plan from the **Plans** tab of your application.

<Warning>
  **Plan Status.** If your app is assigned a plan that has been **Retired**, the dashboard will reject your request for an access token entirely. Always ensure your app is linked to an **Active** or **Archived** plan.
</Warning>

---

## 4. Get an Access Token

Exchange your credentials for a security token using the OAuth 2 client credentials flow.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.trueparser.com/connect/token \
  -H "Content-Type: application/x-www-form-urlencoded" \
  -d "grant_type=client_credentials" \
  -d "client_id=YOUR_CLIENT_ID" \
  -d "client_secret=YOUR_CLIENT_SECRET" \
  -d "scope=TrueParser.API offline_access"
```

---

## 5. Parse a Document

Submit your first document for parsing using the `POST /api/v1/documents/parse` endpoint.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.trueparser.com/api/v1/documents/parse \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_ACCESS_TOKEN" \
  -F "file=@/path/to/document.pdf" \
  -F "documentType=Pdf"
```

---

## 6. Retrieve Results

Once processing is complete, download your structured JSON results.

```bash
curl -X GET https://api.trueparser.com/api/v1/documents/7b1c0d8c-.../result \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_ACCESS_TOKEN"
```
